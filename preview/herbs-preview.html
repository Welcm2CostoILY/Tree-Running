Play through a run

Steps tick themselves in game when you harvest, compost and plant. Here, "Do next step" stands in for that.

What's in it

  • Patches: every herb patch shows whether it's ready, growing (with time left), diseased, dead or empty. It shows when you last saw it and whether it's composted.
  • A herb for each patch: each patch has its own herb, so your best seeds can go in the disease-free patches. "This run needs" adds up the seeds.
  • Checklist: harvest, compost and plant at each stop, in your route order, ticking themselves.
  • Needs: seeds, compost, tools and teleports, against your inventory. It warns when your magic secateurs aren't with you.
  • Profit: each run records the herbs picked at live prices, minus seeds and compost, along with XP. The history works like the tree run's, with delete.
  • Herbs on the way: off by default. It's under Settings in the XP tab.
  • Notification: a RuneLite notification when your herb patches are grown.
