RuneLite settings: Herb runs

For accounts that can't buy seeds and get them from PvM and seed packs. Profit then counts only the harvest, minus compost and payments. "Which herb pays best" ranks herbs by what you pick. The seeds-planted count still shows, just without a price.

What's changing

  • The 274M bug: when you logged in, the plugin briefly read your Farming XP as 0. Your next XP update then looked like one huge gain of all your XP. If you were standing at a grown herb patch, that gain was counted as herbs picked. The fix:
    • It ignores the XP reading at login.
    • It never counts more than a few herbs from one XP drop.
    • It removes history entries with impossible numbers, so your lifetime profit is right again.
    Use the third demo button to compare.
  • Seeds in profit: each run's profit now lists the seeds you planted, how many and what they cost, beside the harvest value, compost and payments. "Which herb pays best" shows the seed cost as its own column. Ironman accounts can turn seed costs off with the setting above.
  • One allotment entry per location: Hosidius, Catherby and the rest show a herb patch and an allotment entry, not North-east and South-west. The allotment checklist covers both patches together ("Plant 6 Snape grass seeds"), and it ticks once both are done.
